The Problem

What PS-16 actually asks for

The Indian Army's drone fleet has scaled across vendors, formations, and theatres faster than its command stack. PS-16 calls for a single integrated platform — and it sets a high bar.

DISC-14 · PS-16

Integrated Drone Management System for Monitoring & Decision Support

3Secure mesh networkSelf-healing, redundant comms across the C2 ↔ GCS ↔ relay ↔ edge chain, with end-to-end AES-256 encryption.

On-Premise Deployment

Every byte stays inside your perimeter.

Trishuleye is engineered for fully on-premise installation at the client's premises. No internet, no OEM cloud, no third-party CDN at runtime — the platform, the AI models, the audit ledger, and the cryptographic material all live behind the air-gap.

Trishuleye on-premise deployment at an Indian Army installation — secure perimeter, four tiers, AES-256-GCM at every internal hop.
AIR-GAPPED
No external runtime dependencies
No internet, OEM cloud, or third-party CDN at runtime. Default — not a configuration option.
ENCRYPTED
AES-256-GCM at every internal hop
C2 ↔ Audit ↔ GCS ↔ MANET ↔ Edge — authenticated and encrypted across all four tiers.
AUDITED
Append-only audit ledger inside perimeter
Cryptographically chained, sub-second replication. Tamper-evident across the C2 / GCS tier.
DEPLOYABLE
Vehicle-borne or fixed-site rack
Same software image, two form-factors. Containerised mobile GCS for brigades; rack-mounted for Corps HQ.
ROLL-UP
Optional one-way data diode
Sanitised situational summary to higher HQ network — outbound only, no return path. Air-gap preserved.
