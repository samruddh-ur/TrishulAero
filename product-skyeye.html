02 // Detection Capabilities

See every drone. Emit nothing.

SkyEye listens rather than transmits — invisible to the threat while giving operators the earliest possible warning across the full RF spectrum.

PASSIVE · ZERO-EMISSION

Invisible to the threat.

SkyEye detects purely by listening to radio-frequency signals — it transmits nothing, so it cannot be located, jammed pre-emptively, or triggered as a beacon. Early warning, without giving away the position of the site it protects.

  • Up to 35 km radius · 360° coverage · zero emission
  • Drone, controller, FPV and telemetry link detection
  • Direction and location finding on every track
AI FINGERPRINTING

The largest library — plus everything not in it.

A 450+ signature library of drones, controllers, FPV and telemetry devices — expanded monthly — is backed by edge-computed AI fingerprinting that flags new and unknown airframes not yet catalogued. Electronic friend-vs-foe discrimination cuts false alarms to near zero.

  • 450+ signatures · updated monthly
  • 99.9% identification & classification accuracy
  • AI detection of unknown / novel drones on-edge
03 // Deployment

Where SkyEye stands watch.

A lightweight all-in-one unit on a tripod or vehicle mount, live in minutes, with cloud multi-site coverage from any smartphone, tablet or computer.
