02 // Mitigation Capabilities

Defend the moment a drone is detected.

SkyGuard acts only on confirmed hostile targets, cued by passive detection and IFF — then overwhelms the drone's control and navigation links across the full 360° airspace.

OVERWHELMING ADVANTAGE

Wins even at the controller.

A jammer-to-signal distance ratio exceeding 20:1 lets SkyGuard defeat a drone even when it is right next to its remote controller — the close-in case where lower-power handheld jammers fail. Multiband coverage spans the consumer and commercial ISM frequencies used by the majority of drone links.

  • Exceeds 20:1 J/S (tested on DJI Mavic 2, CE/SRRC/MIC)
  • Full 360° defence · up to 3 km radius
  • Consumer & commercial ISM multiband coverage
SMART & AUTONOMOUS

Hands-off, but never indiscriminate.

SkyGuard runs 24/7 autonomous defence, triggered only by confirmed hostile targets from paired passive detection and IFF. Near-zero false-alarm gating means it engages threats — not friendly or unrelated signals — with no operator required in the loop.

  • 24/7 autonomous, detection-cued engagement
  • IFF-gated · acts on confirmed hostiles only
  • All-in-one unit · fixed-site or mobile install
